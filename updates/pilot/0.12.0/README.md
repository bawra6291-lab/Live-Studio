# Live Desk / DARSHANFLOW 0.12.0

Adapted the new controls from the uploaded studio into the existing Live Desk workspace.

## Update

When live/recording are idle, pause automation and open Updates → Check for updates → Download & install. Your saved live connections, schedule, camera steps, photos, drafts and credentials remain on this PC.

## New controls

- Publish → Choose photos: 7 portrait, 10 landscape, 17 mixed, or both posts (7 + 10). The destination cards update to the selected section. Facebook now supports a selected 7-photo/10-photo post or two separate posts as well as its mixed 17-photo post.
- For a mixed set, Instagram and YouTube prepare two complete posts (7 + 10); Telegram prepares two albums. The mapping is stated above the destination cards. No photos are silently dropped.
- Review all connected accounts builds saved drafts for verified Facebook, Instagram and Telegram accounts and available YouTube photo handoffs. Review every account, full caption and ordered photo set. Publish reviewed API posts sends API posts in order and prepares YouTube handoffs. Publish the YouTube photo posts yourself, then paste their actual links in the existing handoff controls.
- Dispatch progress shows local preparation and observed post states. History retains returned platform receipts and links. If a batch fails, successful posts remain, the uncertain item requires review and unattempted items return to draft. A started batch is not automatically repeated or resumed after restart. Saved reviews are above the destination cards.
- Download selected photos makes one ordered ZIP with photos, full caption and an order manifest. Both posts uses separate portrait and landscape folders. Downloads do not publish anything.
- WhatsApp photos prepares the selected full photo set plus caption for a supported native share sheet, or ordered download/copy fallback. Finish sending in WhatsApp and check the destination there. Existing 8-community + channel link-sharing controls remain separate and require manual sending/confirmation.
- Caption date uses IST. Add / update date in title keeps your title text, description and hashtags, and replaces only a date added by this button.
- Photos show Reference and Today separately. Click a slot to replace its current photo; the original reference stays intact. Swap supports all 17 positions. Only actual uploaded photos can be exported or published.

Existing Live Desk colours, reference data, Instagram direct login, Facebook/YouTube credentials, Telegram connection and live controls are reused. Images fit without cropping; no random match percentage is shown. Telegram captions remain limited to 1024 characters (emoji may count as two). YouTube/WhatsApp assisted actions are not claimed as automatic publishing.

No real social post, message, livestream, OBS action or camera movement was performed during validation. Test the first real posts by reviewing your accounts and photos in the app.
