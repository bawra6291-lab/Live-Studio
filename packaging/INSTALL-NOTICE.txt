LIVE DESK PILOT

Pause automation and finish or manually take over any live/recording before installing. Close the old Live Desk window. Do not run two versions at once.

This installer bundles Python, Tk and the required dependencies. It installs for the current Windows user without requesting administrator access. OBS administrator setup remains a separate, explicit action.

Existing Windows credentials, settings and journal are retained. Uninstall removes this app installation, not your saved settings or account credentials. Keep your current data backup.

This pilot installer is not code-signed. It is for supervised validation, not a claim of production readiness. Do not promote it to a public stable release until signing, Google/Meta approvals and the real-PC acceptance tests are complete.
